Cosmic Supremacy: Resurgence v0.6.0: messages between players, map colours
that stay, and a fair start for new players.

If you have v0.5.0, the launcher offers this update and installs it for you.
Multiplayer galaxies still accept v0.5.0, so you can update when it suits you.

## New in v0.6.0

**Messages between players.** In a multiplayer galaxy you can write to the
civilizations you have met. A message arrives with the next turn, and the
reply comes back the same way. Your news, messages included, is sent only to
you.

**Your map colours stay.** The colour you give a rival, and whether its
territory and ships show on the map, are kept from turn to turn.

**Admirals on fleets.** An admiral you assign to a fleet stays assigned and
commands it.

**A fair start for new players.** A player who joins a running galaxy starts
with a score of 0, picks their own civilization name and traits, and sets up
their own homeworld.

**Multiplayer by account.** Galaxies are played by account only. A guest can
still play Single Player, and the galaxies an install joined as a guest come
with it when you log in. Logging in or out while the game is closed now works
instead of being refused.

**Community guidelines.** The site has
[community guidelines](https://cosmicresurgence.com/support_guidelines.php.html),
linked when you join a galaxy. The game is for players aged 13 and up.

## Updating from v0.5.0

When you start the launcher, it downloads v0.6.0 and shows **Update to
v0.6.0**. Close the game, click it, and the launcher restarts into v0.6.0,
keeping your `data` folder where it is.

## Install

1. Download `CosmicSupremacy-Resurgence-v0.6.0.zip` below.
2. Unzip the whole folder somewhere you can write to. Desktop or Downloads is
   fine. Not Program Files, and not from inside the zip.
3. Run `CosmicSupremacyLauncher.exe` and pick a mode.

Leave the launcher open while you play. It runs the local game server, and
for Multiplayer it is what sends your turn.

Windows may still show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because a
new signature takes time to earn Windows' trust. Click **More info**, then click **Run anyway**.

## Verifying the download

    SHA-256  (added once the release is built)

    Get-FileHash CosmicSupremacy-Resurgence-v0.6.0.zip -Algorithm SHA256

## Reporting a problem

On the Log tab, press **Send Log** and copy the log reference it shows. Open an
[issue](https://github.com/rsfutch77/Cosmic-Supremacy-Resurgence/issues) with
the reference and what you were doing.
