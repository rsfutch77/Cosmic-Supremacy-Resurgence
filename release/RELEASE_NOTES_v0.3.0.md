Cosmic Supremacy: Resurgence v0.3.0: accounts, a much stronger Single Player
opponent, and Single Player games you can save and continue.

If you have v0.2.2, replace it with this. Your multiplayer empire carries
over; see "Updating from v0.2.2" below.

## New in v0.3.0

**Accounts.** Press **Log in** to create an account with your email address,
or to log in with Google. An account plays your galaxies from any copy of the
game, on any PC. Creating one from this copy keeps the galaxies it has
already joined. Playing as a guest, with no account, still works.

An email account has to verify its address before it joins or plays a
galaxy. Open the link in the email the launcher sends, then press
**I have verified it**.

**A stronger Single Player opponent.** The Resurgence AI now declares war,
invades, and defends its planets. It scans before it attacks, repairs damaged
ships at a shipyard, builds turrets on its borders, gathers its fleet before
sending it, and pays for its ships with the resources they cost.

**Single Player saves and continues.** **Play** continues your newest game,
and **New Game** starts over on a freshly built galaxy. The game saves itself
before each turn resolves, and **Save** asks for a name. Turns happen only
when you press **Next Turn**, so you can take as long as you like.

**The Tutorial** has corrected steps and texts, and the launcher marks it
Completed once you finish it. The game window opens larger.

**Governors and coats of arms** you save in the game are kept.

**Multiplayer** carries more of what you do in a turn: switching ship designs
off and on, and choosing what a planet builds. A new empire keeps the
homeworld and the civilisation traits you choose for it. [verify]

**A privacy page** on the site says what the game keeps about you:
[cosmicresurgence.com/support_privacy.php.html](https://cosmicresurgence.com/support_privacy.php.html).

## Updating from v0.2.2

1. Unzip v0.3.0 into a new folder.
2. Copy the `data` folder from your v0.2.2 folder into the new one, before you
   start the new launcher. It holds the sign-in your multiplayer empire
   belongs to. Without it, the new launcher joins as a new player and cannot
   play your empire.
3. Start the new launcher, and delete the old folder when you are done with it.

To move your empire to an account, start v0.3.0 with your old `data` folder
and press **Log in**, then **Create account**. The account keeps the empire.

Single Player games saved in v0.2 [verify: carry over, or start a New Game].

## Install

1. Download `CosmicSupremacy-Resurgence-v0.3.0.zip` below.
2. Unzip the whole folder somewhere you can write to. Desktop or Downloads is
   fine. Not Program Files, and not from inside the zip.
3. Run `CosmicSupremacyLauncher.exe` and pick a mode.

Leave the launcher open while you play. It runs the local game server, and
for Multiplayer it is what sends your turn.

Windows may still show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because a
new signature takes time to earn Windows' trust. Click **More info**, then click **Run anyway**.

On the Log tab, press **Send Log** and copy the log reference it shows. Open an
[issue](https://github.com/rsfutch77/Cosmic-Supremacy-Resurgence/issues) with
the reference and what you were doing.
