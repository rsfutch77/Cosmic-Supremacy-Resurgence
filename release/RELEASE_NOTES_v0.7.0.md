Cosmic Supremacy: Resurgence v0.7.0: more of your turn carries over in
multiplayer, a fairer view of the galaxy, and news in the launcher.

If you have v0.6.0, the launcher offers this update and installs it for you.
Multiplayer galaxies still accept v0.6.0, so you can update when it suits you.

## New in v0.7.0

**More of your turn carries over.** In a multiplayer galaxy, these now last
into the next turn instead of being undone: selling a facility, retiring
stationed soldiers, editing, renaming or deleting a ship design, setting
hyperspace energy links, connecting wormholes, deleting scan reports and
editing notes.

**Your copy of the galaxy holds only what you can see.** Rivals' treasuries,
research and planet details are no longer sent to you.

**Vote to end a galaxy.** The **Vote to End the Galaxy** box on the Overview
screen now counts. The galaxy's operator sees the votes and decides.

**News in the launcher.** A line under Multiplayer carries the latest news,
and the site's home page lists news and the galaxies that are running.

**A fair start, completed.** A player who joins a running galaxy now also
starts with no reputation of their own.

## Known issues

- Carrying out a scan in multiplayer is still undone at the next turn.

## Updating from v0.6.0

When you start the launcher, it downloads v0.7.0 and shows **Update to
v0.7.0**. Close the game, click it, and the launcher restarts into v0.7.0,
keeping your `data` folder where it is.

## Install

1. Download `CosmicSupremacy-Resurgence-v0.7.0.zip` below.
2. Unzip the whole folder somewhere you can write to. Desktop or Downloads is
   fine. Not Program Files, and not from inside the zip.
3. Run `CosmicSupremacyLauncher.exe` and pick a mode.

Leave the launcher open while you play. It runs the local game server, and
for Multiplayer it is what sends your turn.

Windows may still show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because a
new signature takes time to earn Windows' trust. Click **More info**, then click **Run anyway**.

## Verifying the download

    SHA-256  <filled in after the signed build>

    Get-FileHash CosmicSupremacy-Resurgence-v0.7.0.zip -Algorithm SHA256

## Reporting a problem

On the Log tab, press **Send Log** and copy the log reference it shows. Open an
[issue](https://github.com/rsfutch77/Cosmic-Supremacy-Resurgence/issues) with
the reference and what you were doing. To report a player, send a private
message to an admin of the
[Cosmic Supremacy Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/145339627564).
