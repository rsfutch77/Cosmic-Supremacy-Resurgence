Cosmic Supremacy: Resurgence v0.7.2

If you have v0.7.1, the launcher offers this update and installs it for you.
Multiplayer galaxies still accept v0.7.1, so you can update when it suits you.

## Fixed in v0.7.2

This was fixed on the multiplayer server, so every launcher has it already.

**Each galaxy offers only its own coats of arms.** The Coat-of-Arms box
showed a coat of arms you had uploaded in another galaxy, and kept showing it.
Now it is empty until you upload one in this galaxy. To use the same image in
two galaxies, upload it in each.

A coat of arms you drag into the box is kept only when you press **OK**.
**Decide Later** leaves the box empty, and the box asks again next time.

## Known issues

- Carrying out a scan in multiplayer is still undone at the next turn.
- A coat of arms cannot be changed after your empire is named.

## Updating from v0.7.1

When you start the launcher, it downloads v0.7.2 and shows **Update to
v0.7.2**. Close the game, click it, and the launcher restarts into v0.7.2,
keeping your `data` folder where it is.

## Install

1. Download `CosmicSupremacy-Resurgence-v0.7.2.zip` below.
2. Unzip the whole folder somewhere you can write to. Desktop or Downloads is
   fine. Not Program Files, and not from inside the zip.
3. Run `CosmicSupremacyLauncher.exe` and pick a mode.

Leave the launcher open while you play. It runs the local game server, and
for Multiplayer it is what sends your turn.

Windows may still show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because a
new signature takes time to earn Windows' trust. Click **More info**, then click **Run anyway**.
