Cosmic Supremacy: Resurgence v0.8.1

If you have v0.8.0, the launcher offers this update and installs it for you.
Multiplayer galaxies still accept v0.8.0, so you can update when it suits you.

## Fixed in v0.8.1

**The replay opens once a galaxy has ended.** As in the original game, a
galaxy's replay is for after it ends. v0.8.0 offered **Replay** on galaxies
still running. The multiplayer server now refuses the replay until the galaxy
ends, for every launcher, and the launcher shows **Replay** only on ended
galaxies.

## Known issues

- A coat of arms cannot be changed after your empire is named.

## Updating from v0.8.0

When you start the launcher, it downloads v0.8.1 and shows **Update to
v0.8.1**. Close the game, click it, and the launcher restarts into v0.8.1,
keeping your `data` folder where it is.

## Install

1. Download `CosmicSupremacy-Resurgence-v0.8.1.zip` below.
2. Unzip the whole folder somewhere you can write to. Desktop or Downloads is
   fine. Not Program Files, and not from inside the zip.
3. Run `CosmicSupremacyLauncher.exe` and pick a mode.

Leave the launcher open while you play. It runs the local game server, and
for Multiplayer it is what sends your turn.

The first time you run the launcher, Windows may still show "Windows protected
your PC", because a new signature takes time to earn Windows' trust. Click
**More info** and check that the publisher is Robert Futch, then click **Run
anyway**. On Windows 11 there is no need to turn off Smart App Control.

## Verifying the download

    SHA-256  [filled in after the build]
