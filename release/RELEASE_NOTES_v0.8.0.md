Cosmic Supremacy: Resurgence v0.8.0: scans that last in multiplayer, the
replay, and a view of the galaxy closer to the original's.

If you have v0.7.2, the launcher offers this update and installs it for you.
Multiplayer galaxies still accept v0.7.2, so you can update when it suits you,
but scans and the replay need v0.8.0.

## New in v0.8.0

**Scans last in multiplayer.** A scan you carry out in a multiplayer galaxy is
now run by the galaxy's server, as in the original game, and its report stays
with you into the next turn. While the answer is on its way the launcher shows
a small window. If it takes too long, **Retry** cancels the scan so you can
run it again, and a cancelled scan is never charged.

**Watch the replay.** The game's replay screen now plays back the turns of a
multiplayer galaxy. In a galaxy that was already running, it starts with the
turns played just before this update.

**You see what the original server showed you.** Rivals' treaties, idle
admirals, unused ship designs, scan reports and votes are no longer sent to
you.

**More of your turn carries over.** These now last into the next turn: a lump
sum of credits paid to another empire, a ship's course through a connected
wormhole, a wormhole an ally connects by treaty, and your News screen
settings.

**Join before the start.** Joining a galaxy that has not started yet puts your
empire in it within a few minutes, so you can set up your first turn before
the galaxy starts.

**The launcher's Galaxies list** shows a galaxy that has not started yet as
**starting**, and counts players still joining, as in "4 (3 joining)".

## Fixed in v0.8.0

- A ship design with no scanner closed the game on the Ship Design tab. Such
  a design is now refused.
- The game's window was small on large screens. It now opens sized to your
  screen the first time you play, and always in the Tutorial. If you have
  played before, hold Ctrl as the game starts to get the new size.

## Known issues

- A coat of arms cannot be changed after your empire is named.

## Updating from v0.7.2

When you start the launcher, it downloads v0.8.0 and shows **Update to
v0.8.0**. Close the game, click it, and the launcher restarts into v0.8.0,
keeping your `data` folder where it is.

## Install

1. Download `CosmicSupremacy-Resurgence-v0.8.0.zip` below.
2. Unzip the whole folder somewhere you can write to. Desktop or Downloads is
   fine. Not Program Files, and not from inside the zip.
3. Run `CosmicSupremacyLauncher.exe` and pick a mode.

Leave the launcher open while you play. It runs the local game server, and
for Multiplayer it is what sends your turn.

The first time you run the launcher, Windows may still show "Windows protected
your PC", because a new signature takes time to earn Windows' trust. Click
**More info** and check that the publisher is Robert Futch, then click **Run
anyway**. On Windows 11 there is no need to turn off Smart App Control.

On the Log tab, press **Send Log** and copy the log reference it shows. Open an
[issue](https://github.com/rsfutch77/Cosmic-Supremacy-Resurgence/issues) with
the reference and what you were doing. To report a player, send a private
message to an admin of the
[Cosmic Supremacy Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/145339627564).
