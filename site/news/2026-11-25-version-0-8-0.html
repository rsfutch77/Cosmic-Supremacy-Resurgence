Resurgence v0.8.0

Version 0.8.0 of the launcher is out. If you already have the launcher, it offers the update the next time you start it.

Scans now work in multiplayer. A scan is run by the galaxy's server, as in the original game, and its report stays with you into the next turn. While the answer is on its way the launcher shows a small window, and a scan that is given up on is never charged. Ships now travel through connected wormholes, and a wormhole an ally connects by treaty is linked for you too.

The game's replay screen plays back the turns of a multiplayer galaxy, and rivals' treaties, idle admirals, unused designs and scan reports are no longer sent to you, as in the original.

An older launcher still plays, but scans and the replay need v0.8.0, so update before your next scan.
