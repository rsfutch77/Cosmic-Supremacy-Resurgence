Galaxy One starts this Saturday

Galaxy One starts on Saturday 28 November at noon Mountain time (19:00 UTC), with turns every 2 hours. Everyone who joins before then starts on the same turn, so there is still time to join from the launcher's Multiplayer page and set up your first turn.

It is the first regular galaxy with scans and wormhole travel working, so update to launcher v0.8.0 before it starts.
