Resurgence v0.7.0

Version 0.7.0 of the launcher is out. If you already have the launcher, it offers the update the next time you start it.

In multiplayer, more of what you do in a turn now carries over to the next: selling a facility, retiring stationed soldiers, editing, renaming or deleting a ship design, setting hyperspace energy links, connecting wormholes, deleting scan reports and editing notes. Carrying out a scan is still undone at the next turn.

Your copy of the galaxy now holds only what your own client shows you. Rivals' treasuries, research and planet details are no longer in it.

The Vote to End the Galaxy box on the Overview screen now counts. The galaxy's operator sees the votes and decides.

The Galaxies panel on this site now lists the galaxies that are running.

To report a player, or to ask for your data or its deletion, send a private message to an admin of the Cosmic Supremacy Facebook group.
