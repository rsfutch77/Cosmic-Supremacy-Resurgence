Galaxy One starts Saturday

Galaxy One, the first regular galaxy, is open to join now and starts on Saturday 28 November at noon Mountain time (19:00 UTC). Turns close every 2 hours from then on.

It is a small galaxy of 27 star systems, with room for 20 empires and one computer empire, Baron. Everyone who joins before Saturday starts on the same turn, so there is no head start to catch up on. Join from the launcher's Multiplayer page; it needs launcher v0.7.2 or later, and an older launcher offers the update when it starts.

Mini Sandbox and The Sandbox keep running alongside it.
