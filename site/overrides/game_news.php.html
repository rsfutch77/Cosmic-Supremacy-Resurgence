Welcome to ResurgencePosted on 13.09.2026

Cosmic Supremacy shut down and its website went offline, taking with it a game that a lot of people spent years playing. Resurgence is an effort to bring it back.

The work is open source. Progress, current issues, and everyone who has contributed are on GitHub:
https://github.com/rsfutch77/Cosmic-Supremacy-Resurgence
