How a multiplayer turn works

In Cosmic Supremacy: Resurgence every player in a galaxy plays the same turn at the same time. The game no longer runs the turn on your computer: a referee runs it for the whole galaxy at the deadline.

Playing your turn

  • Press Play on a galaxy in the launcher. It opens the game on the current turn, as your empire sees it.
  • Give your orders as you always would: build, research, move your ships, write to other players.
  • The launcher saves your turn while you play and sends it to the galaxy during the turn and again at the deadline. Closing the game sends it too.
  • You can open the game again before the deadline and carry on from where you left off.
At the deadline

  • The referee takes every player's orders, checks each one against the game's rules, and runs the turn.
  • An order the game would not have allowed is left out, and the launcher tells you which.
  • Press Play again to open the next turn.
Between turns

  • Each galaxy sets its own turn length. The launcher shows the time left.
  • A scan is answered by the referee within seconds, while you wait.
  • Messages to other players arrive with the next turn.
If you miss turns

  • Your empire carries on without new orders.
  • After many missed turns in a row, its seat may be taken back so that someone else can play.
