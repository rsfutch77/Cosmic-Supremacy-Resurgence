Community Guidelines

Cosmic Supremacy: Resurgence is a volunteer revival of the game, run without profit. These guidelines apply in every galaxy it runs: in the messages players send each other, and in the names given to empires, planets, systems, ships and fleets. The game is for players aged 13 and up.

Diplomacy, alliances, betrayal and war are part of the game. These guidelines are about how players treat each other, not about how they play.

Treat other players with respect

  • No harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content or personal attacks, in messages or in names.
  • Do not share another player's personal information, or ask for it.
  • Do not impersonate another player or the people who run the project.
Play fair

  • Play with the launcher and the game as released. Do not modify the game, edit the turns it sends, or use a program that plays for you or shows what the game hides.
  • If you find a bug that gives an advantage, report it instead of using it.
  • Do not attack or overload the game's servers, and do not try to reach another player's account.
Reporting a problem

To report a player, a message, a name or suspected cheating, open an issue at https://github.com/rsfutch77/Cosmic-Supremacy-Resurgence/issues. Name the galaxy, the turn and the empire, and describe what happened. Do not paste anyone's personal information or private messages into the issue; say that you have them, and we will ask for them privately.

Every report is answered, even when no action follows.

What happens

Reports are checked against the galaxy's turns, which the server keeps. What follows depends on what happened, and is explained to the player concerned:

  • a warning;
  • a message or name removed, or a name changed;
  • the player's messages blocked for a time;
  • the player's empire removed from the galaxy;
  • the player's sign-in disabled.

Penalties last for a set time where they can. Repeated problems are recorded and treated more seriously.
