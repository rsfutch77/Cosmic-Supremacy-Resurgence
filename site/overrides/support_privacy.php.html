Privacy

Cosmic Supremacy: Resurgence is a volunteer revival of the game, run without profit. This page says what the game and this site keep about you, who can see it, and how long it is kept. It is meant for players aged 13 and up.

Playing on your own PC

The Tutorial, the Demo and Single Player run entirely on your PC. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Playing online

Each copy of the launcher signs in to our server as a guest, with a random identifier and no name or email. The first characters of that identifier are your player code, shown under the launcher's title. When you play a galaxy, the server keeps:

  • your player name, your civilisation's name and the names you give things in the game, which other players in the galaxy may see;
  • the messages you send in the game. They travel inside the galaxy's turns, which every player in it receives, and the game shows each one only to the players it was sent to;
  • your turns, which the server keeps for 18 turns, and the galaxy's own turns, which every player in it receives;
  • your request to join a galaxy, and any warning or penalty the galaxy's operator records.

An account, if you make one

You can play as a guest for as long as you like. If you create an account, or log in with Google, the account holds your email address. It is used to log you in and to send you account emails, such as the link that verifies your address or resets your password, and nothing else. Other players never see it. Your password is held by Google's sign-in service, Firebase Authentication, and never reaches us. With a Google login, Google gives the sign-in service your email address and basic profile, such as your name. The launcher uses only the email address.

Your launcher's log

If something goes wrong, the launcher can send its log so it can be fixed. It is sent only when you press Send, and the launcher tells you what it holds first. Your Windows user name and your email address are taken out of it. A sent log is kept for 14 days.

What we do not collect

No age, no location, no payment details. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, on this site or in the game.

Who can see it

Other players see what the game shows them: names, civilisations and the messages sent to them. The people who run the project can see everything listed above, and use it only to run the game and fix problems. The server runs on Google Cloud and Firebase, which store it for us. Google's servers also record the internet address each request comes from, as every web service does.

Asking for your data, or for it to be deleted

Open an issue on the project's GitHub with your player code, and say what you would like. Issues there are public, so do not put your email address or password in one. Your civilisation's name and messages are part of past turns that every player in the galaxy already has, so they cannot be taken back out of those; they can be renamed in turns to come, and everything else above can be deleted.

Changes

This page changes when the game starts keeping something new. Last updated 22 October 2026.
