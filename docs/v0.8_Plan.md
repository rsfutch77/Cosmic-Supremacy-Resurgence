## 4. Original features that do not work yet

- Report, "The original client's online path": **A scan is not carried** (after §2). Online
  through the relay, the referee running the client's own scan, in this order:
  1. decode the request (`0x00576160`) and each report's wire form (`0x0054E2D0`);
  2. a referee call that loads the galaxy, replays the civ's earlier scans this turn, runs
     `UseScans` and returns the reports, the scanner's `OWPR` and its `KNPL`;
  3. a relay route that files the request and returns the answer, and a referee worker that
     picks it up within seconds;
  4. the tick replays the turn's delivered scans, so the stock, the reports and the
     detection items land in the authoritative galaxy. A scan counts as delivered only once
     the launcher has handed its answer to the client and said so; a scan given up on is
     cancelled and never charged, even if its answer comes later;
  5. a launcher endpoint that speaks the frame protocol and forwards `DOSC`, and shows a
     scan-pending window above the client until the answer is sent or it gives up. Giving up,
     by the player's button or the launcher's limit, closes the socket so the client ends its
     wait; a client still stuck after that is restarted on the current turn from the window;
  6. the production client patch, with the client's 20-second wait raised past the
     launcher's own limit;
  7. tests for all eight scan types, since the patch sends every scan online: each run
     through the referee and compared with the client's own offline report, and a
     multiplayer checklist row for each, seen on screen (Robert, 19 November 2026).
- Report, "The original client's online path": **Notes
  and wormhole links are carried as read, not measured**; **The replay is not served**;
  **People on another civ's planet or ship are refused**.
- Report, "The `NEWS` record and player messages": **Messages arrive between ticks, close to
  instantly**.
- Report §14: **Peace, alliances, expiry and the sharing items are not measured**.
- Multiplayer Checklist **GAG-Cog-B5** and **GAG-Cog-A4**: the Tutorial starts, completes and
  can be replayed from the launcher.
