Before you join the beta
========================

Cosmic Supremacy: Resurgence is a rebuild of a game from 2008. The beta runs as
one galaxy, kept going by one person on one PC. It works, and there are a few
things about it you should know before you spend weeks in a galaxy. None of
them are faults we are hiding. They are choices, and they are written down here
so you meet them here rather than the hard way.


Your name is not a password
---------------------------

You pick a name when you join, and that name is the whole of your identity.
There is no password and no account. Nothing stops another player typing your
name, and nothing proves that the person playing your empire is you.

Cheating is possible, and it is easy. We are not going to pretend otherwise,
and in this beta we are not trying to stop it. If somebody is spoiling your
game, tell us and we will sort it out by hand.

Proper accounts are the next thing being built. They are not here yet.


Your computer is sent the whole galaxy
--------------------------------------

At the start of each turn your copy of the game receives the entire state of
the galaxy: every player, every planet, every ship. The game shows you only
what you are meant to see, and an unmodified copy hides the rest properly.

A modified copy would not. Anyone who alters the game can see the whole map,
including where your fleets are and what is on your planets. Nothing on our
side prevents that, because the hiding is done by the game on each player's own
machine.

So play accordingly. Nothing in this galaxy is really a secret.


Turns close whether or not you played
-------------------------------------

A turn lasts four hours. When the clock runs out, the turn is worked out from
whatever has been handed in by then. If you did not play, your empire does
nothing that turn except grow. The galaxy does not wait for you, and the turn
is not replayed later.


If you stop playing, your empire is deleted
-------------------------------------------

Miss [SET BEFORE THE BETA OPENS: warn_after_misses, the number of missed
turns before a warning] turns in a row and the launcher will warn you.

Miss [SET BEFORE THE BETA OPENS: reclaim_after_misses, the number of missed
turns before deletion] turns in a row and your empire is removed from the
galaxy. Your planets go back to being unowned and can be settled by anyone,
your ships are gone, and your name stops working in that galaxy.

Nobody inherits what you built. It is not handed to another player and it is
not taken over by the computer. It stops existing.

This is deliberate. A permanent galaxy filling up with abandoned empires that
nobody is steering is not a game anyone wants to play.


The galaxy can be ended at any time
-----------------------------------

There is one galaxy, and it runs until it is stopped. There is no season, no
end date and no scheduled finish. Whoever is running the beta can end it
whenever they decide to, including in the middle of your best game, and you may
not get any notice.

Nothing you build here is permanent. Treat it as a beta rather than somewhere
to settle.


What leaves your computer
-------------------------

Three things, and nothing else is collected.

1. Your turns. Playing a multiplayer turn sends your saved game to wherever the
   galaxy is kept. That is the game working.

2. An anonymous id. The first time the launcher connects, it registers an
   anonymous id so the galaxy can tell one installation from another. It is not
   linked to your name, your email or anything about you, and you never see it.

3. A copy of the launcher's log, if there is a problem. The launcher writes a
   log of what it did, on your machine. When something goes wrong we may need
   it, and the launcher can write out a copy made fit to send.
   [SET BEFORE THE BETA OPENS: log_upload, whether that copy is sent
   automatically or only when you choose to send it]

Your Windows account name is taken out of the file paths in that copy before it
is written. Here is what is still in it:

  - Your player name, which is also the name of your empire.
  - Which galaxy you are playing and where it is kept. For a galaxy shared over
    a home network, that includes the name of the machine hosting it.
  - For each time the game saved, the name of the game, the turn number and
    what the game asked for.
  - Which parts of the game were started, which stopped, and how they stopped.
  - The folder layout of your installation, with your account name replaced and
    the rest of each path left readable. A folder you named after yourself is
    still readable.
  - In single player, the whole of the computer opponent's reasoning, which
    names planets, ships and research, and says nothing about your machine.
  - The time, to the second, of all of the above.

Two things are deliberately not removed, and it is better that you hear them
from us. If the galaxy is kept on another machine on a network, that machine's
name stays in the path. And if your Windows account name appears somewhere that
is not a file path, it is left alone.

The copy has a size limit. If the log is longer than that, the oldest part is
dropped and the most recent part is kept, because the most recent part is where
the problem is.

You can read the copy before you send it. It is a plain text file and it is
written next to the launcher's own log.
