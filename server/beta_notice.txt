Before you join the beta
========================

- Please don't cheat on purpose, and treat other players with respect:
https://cosmicresurgence.com/support_guidelines.php.html
- If you miss a bunch of days, your empire may be removed
- The galaxy may be restarted at any time while we fix bugs
- There may be game breaking bugs, submit them here:
https://github.com/rsfutch77/Cosmic-Supremacy-Resurgence
- The game is for players aged 13 and up
- What the game keeps about you, and for how long:
https://cosmicresurgence.com/support_terms.php.html#privacy
